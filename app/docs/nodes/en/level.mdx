# Level

<DemoNode type="level"/>

## What does it do?
Shifts the dark or light tones of an image.

## Why is it needed?
As an example, we can take grayscale manga in which the black color is gray. This node is just the thing for fixing such mishaps.
<br/> You can pick and apply the desired settings in the <code><T k="home-page.levels-preview" /></code> tab in the code section.
{/* todo: insert a screenshot with an example */}

## Parameters
* <code><T k="nodes.level.low-input"/></code> — enhances dark tones (dark becomes darker);
* <code><T k="nodes.level.high-input"/></code> — enhances light tones (light becomes lighter);
* <code><T k="nodes.level.gamma"/></code> — shifts mid-tones;
* <code><T k="nodes.level.low-output"/></code> — reduces dark tones (dark becomes lighter);
* <code><T k="nodes.level.high-output"/></code> — reduces light tones (light becomes darker).
