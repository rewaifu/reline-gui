# Grayscale Manga Upscaling Basics

The choice of approach depends on your input:
* For grayscale scans without screentone, everything is relatively simple — you can use either general models for color, or, for sharper lines, one of the DS models (which may have a detrimental effect on various textures), or `4x_eula_digimanga_MiA_65k`.
* With scans that have screentone, things are a bit more complicated.

## There are several paradigms for upscaling scans with screentone:

### 1. MangaScale

The most primitive in essence, since we only need to use the <NodeRef type="upscale" /> node, but&nbsp;it only applies to sources with a sufficiently well-visible screentone.
<br/>More in <ArticleRef slug="mangascale" />

### 2. Screentone reapplication
A more complex algorithm that converts the original screentone into shades of gray and then reapplies it.
