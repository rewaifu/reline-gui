## How does it work?
Special models are used for this paradigm, most often with&nbsp;MS/MangaScale in&nbsp;the name, these include:
* `4x_wtp_MangaScale_GfisrV2` — the most modern and&nbsp;versatile model, will suit&nbsp;most scans;
* The `4x_MangaJanai` family — the previous king of mangascale, known for its sharp lines and&nbsp;not&nbsp;very pleasant artifacts on&nbsp;non-standard screentone;
* `4x_eula_digimanga_bw_v2_cn1_307k` — one&nbsp;of&nbsp;the first MS models, now&nbsp;quite outdated and needed only in&nbsp;very rare cases.

The principle of their work is that&nbsp;they&nbsp;try to improve the&nbsp;screentone that already&nbsp;exists on&nbsp;the images, and&nbsp;when&nbsp;there is none, they start&nbsp;making it&nbsp;up, which&nbsp;most&nbsp;often leads to&nbsp;various artifacts.
